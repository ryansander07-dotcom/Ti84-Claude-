Camera back plate: how to print (camera_plate.3mf)

Printer: needs a 256 mm bed (X1, P1, A1). It's 89 x 193 mm, so it does NOT fit
the A1 mini.

Print the fit test first (camera_plate_fit_test.3mf, about 20 minutes): a 30 mm
slice of the plate with both edge grips. Slide it onto the calculator from the
bottom end. It should slide on snugly and not fall off. The calculator's size here
is an estimate, so if it's loose or won't go on, tell Ryan before printing the
whole plate.

1. Open camera_plate.3mf in Bambu Studio (File > Open, or drag it in).
   It's already in the right orientation: the side that touches the calculator
   faces down, so the outside comes out clean. Don't flip it.
2. Pick your printer and a 0.4 mm nozzle.
3. Filament: PLA or PETG. White or light gray matches the calculator's back.
4. Process: the standard "0.20mm Standard" profile, then change:
     Strength > Wall loops: 3
     Strength > Sparse infill density: 20%
     Support: ON, type "tree(auto)", "On build plate only" is fine
   Supports are needed: the plate floats 4 mm up between the two edge grips, and
   the raised area is hollow underneath. They all sit on the side that faces the
   calculator, so they don't show.
5. Slice and print. Roughly 2-3 hours and about 40 g of filament plus supports.

After printing, pull the supports out of the hollow raised area and from under the
plate. Clean up the edges of the lens window, the USB-C opening and the switch slot
with a hobby knife if needed.

The fit test uses the same settings (supports ON).
