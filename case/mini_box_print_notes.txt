Mini box: how to print (mini_box.3mf)

1. Open mini_box.3mf in Bambu Studio (File > Open, or drag it in).
   Both parts are already laid out flat on one plate, in the right orientation.
   Don't rotate them. If it asks whether to load it as a project, either answer is fine.
2. Pick your printer and a 0.4 mm nozzle.
3. Filament: PLA (or PETG if it'll live in a hot car). White or light gray looks best.
4. Process: the standard "0.20mm Standard" profile, then change:
     Strength > Wall loops: 3
     Strength > Sparse infill density: 20%
     Support: OFF (none needed)
5. Slice and print. About 30-40 minutes and roughly 7 g of filament.

The two parts:
  mini_base   (31.8 x 42.3 x 3.7 mm)   flat side down
  mini_cover  (31.8 x 42.3 x 11.1 mm)  printed upside down, top face on the plate;
              the open side faces up. That's on purpose.

The cover should press onto the base snugly. If it's too tight, sand the outside of
the base's rim a little; if it's loose, a drop of glue holds it.
